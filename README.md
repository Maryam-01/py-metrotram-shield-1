## Metro Tram Company Profile

### Overview

Metro Tram is a mid-sized urban tram operator serving a fictional city of around 800,000 people. It runs 4 tram lines, 20 stops, and carries approximately 120,000 passengers per day. The company is publicly funded but operates semi-independently, and is under constant pressure to modernise its systems while keeping costs down.

The tech team is small, three developers, one data analyst, and a product manager, and they rely heavily on junior developers and contractors to get things shipped. This is where you come in.

### The Team

**Priya Nair**: Head of Technology. Pragmatic and direct. Cares about reliability above all else. Doesn't like over-engineered solutions.

**Dom Farrell**: Senior Developer. Friendly, a bit overstretched. Leaves detailed comments in code and writes good tickets. The person who briefs the students.

**Suki Tanaka**: Data Analyst. Works with ridership data and timetables.
